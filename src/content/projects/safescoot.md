Safescoot.ie is a free, independent tool that helps people in Ireland work out whether their e-scooter is road-legal — and, just as importantly, what the answer means for them. Enter your scooter's specs and get more than a legal-or-not label. See exactly what the result means for you: seizure risk, what it costs to reclaim, and where you stand if you already own one.
