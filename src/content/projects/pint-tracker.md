A good pub is about more than price. It’s the quality of the Guinness, the feel of the room, and whether the place is worth your time. Pint Tracker tries to make that decision clearer.

It’s built independently and shaped by people who actually go out, notice the details and share what they find.

We default to Guinness because virtually every bar in Ireland serves it, which makes it the clearest common reference point. People can still submit other beers, and if a bar doesn’t pour Guinness but does serve Beamish or Murphy’s, we use that as the core price so comparisons stay useful.
