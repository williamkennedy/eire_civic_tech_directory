Finding a rental in Ireland is stressful, expensive, and confusing. Renters waste hours applying for places they can't truly afford — once you add commute costs, utilities, deposit, and move-in expenses, the "affordable" listing often isn't.

Safety data, transport costs, housing support limits, and cost-of-living trends are scattered across hard-to-use government websites. We pull it all together in one place.
