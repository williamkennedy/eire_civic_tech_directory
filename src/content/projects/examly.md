Examly (examly.ie) is an Irish exam-prep website for Leaving Cert and Junior Cycle students. It has 37,000+ exam questions sorted by topic with marking schemes, from Leaving Cert papers 2001 to 2026, Junior Cycle papers 2002 to 2026 and Examly's own mocks, sample and predicted papers. It also has notes, sample essays, flashcards, grinds and AI that marks your answers. 1 in 3 students who sat the Leaving Cert last year used Examly.
