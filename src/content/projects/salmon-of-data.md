Salmon of Data is a collection of data dashboards and trackers by Peter Donaghy, a Belfast-based accountant and data analyst.

The electricity dashboards for Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland show demand, generation and interconnector flows, with the latest readings and more than a decade of historical data. The Northern Ireland elections dashboard covers votes and vote share by party and community in every election from 1921 onwards.

The trackers test hypotheses over time. One follows El Niño from Pacific temperatures to food prices, and another measures whether AI is displacing white-collar jobs. A data calendar lists upcoming releases for each tracker.
