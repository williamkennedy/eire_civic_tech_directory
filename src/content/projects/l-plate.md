The Irish driver theory test (DTT) is a 40-question multiple-choice test taken in 45 minutes. You need 35 correct to pass, and the fee is €45, booked on theorytest.ie. Practise free with L-Plate first. L-Plate is independent and not affiliated with the RSA.
