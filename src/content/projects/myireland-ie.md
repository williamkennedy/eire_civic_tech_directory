MyIreland.ie is a labour of love with one simple idea at its heart: Ireland's history should be something you can see, and something that makes you feel at home. Drag the timeline and ten thousand years sweep across the island — hunter-gatherers, tomb builders, the Five Fifths, Vikings, Normans, plantations, famine, revolution, partition, peace. Tap a county and it tells you its story. Type your surname and the map lights up where your people come from.

And everywhere, the unknown history — the stories that rarely make the schoolbooks: the famine deadlier (per head) than the Great Famine, the boy crowned King of England in Dublin, the Mayo lighthouse reading that saved D-Day, the "notable city" on Ptolemy's map that no one has ever found.
