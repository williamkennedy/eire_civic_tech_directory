At Historic Stats, the aim is to make the intricate details of historical census data accessible and engaging. The platform is designed to provide a user-friendly experience, allowing you to explore and understand the demographic, social, and economic landscapes of Ireland and Wales across the 19th and 20th centuries.

By delving into the intricacies of census data, the goal is to foster a deeper connection with the heritage of Ireland and Wales. Whether you're a researcher, student, history enthusiast, or even discovering your family's past, this website offers a modern look into the society of the past. Historic Stats is not just a set of graphs and maps; it's a journey through time. Join in uncovering the stories embedded in the census data.
