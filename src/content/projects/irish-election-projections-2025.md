The goal of this project is not to create a perfect projection. Instead it aims to create a realistic estimate of results so people can better visualise how STV works and gain a clearer understanding of how votes translate into seats.

Our projections are non-partisan and neutral, we aim to replicate potential results as accurately as possible so have absolutely no interest in making any party appear to be doing better or worse on our website.

Our blog may contain opinions.
