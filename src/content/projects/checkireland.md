CheckIreland is a set of 87 free tools that apply Ireland's published rules, including tax bands, rent caps, grant rates, welfare rates and consumer law, to your own numbers. It shows what something should cost, what you are entitled to and whether a deal is fair before you pay.

The tools cover money, home and property, cars, energy, consumer rights, work and pay, and grants. You can check whether a rent increase is legal, work out take-home pay, test a builder's quote, compare electricity tariffs or find which grants you may qualify for.

Every tool names the official body behind the rules it applies, such as Revenue, the Central Bank, the RTB, SEAI or the CCPC, and states its assumptions. Most calculators run entirely in your browser and need no account.
