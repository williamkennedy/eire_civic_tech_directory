Derelict Property Leads aggregates vacant and derelict property registers from 24 of Ireland's 26 local councils into a single searchable platform. Each listing includes Street View imagery, GPS coordinates, property descriptions, and upfront grant eligibility checks for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant (up to €70,000). Buyers can unlock registered owner details, generate ready-made outreach letters, and track properties through a personal shortlist — giving direct access to housing stock that rarely reaches the open market.
