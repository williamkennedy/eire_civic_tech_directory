Civgraph hosts digital maps of boundaries across the island of Ireland ranging from local government areas to electoral constituencies to geographical features. These maps are gathered from various different organisations and government agencies to be compiled in one place, with some maps being digitised for the first time by the site's team of volunteers. The site also features election data, census data, books and papers and an easy to browse collection of the 1.5+ million records held by the PRONI (Public Record Office of Northern Ireland). Aside from the addition of more maps and data, the site's future goals include giving users the ability to generate election infographics, research politicians' electoral biographies and perform machine learning analysis on a vast collection of data.
