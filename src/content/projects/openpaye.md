A free, open-source salary calculator for the people of Ireland. Estimates only: your payslip depends on your Revenue tax credit certificate. Income Tax, USC and PRSI for 2025, 2026, 2027, including Budget 2027. Free, private, and open source. Nothing you type leaves your device.
