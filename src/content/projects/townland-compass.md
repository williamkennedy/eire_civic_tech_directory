Discover which of Ireland's 60,000+ townlands you're standing in. Works fully offline with compass, map, and Irish place name glossary.

Ireland is divided into over 60,000 townlands — ancient land boundaries that have shaped Irish identity for centuries. Townland Compass tells you which one you're in, right now.
