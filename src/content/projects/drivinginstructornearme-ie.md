Finding a good driving instructor in Ireland usually means asking around and hoping for the best. Knowing who is licensed to teach tells you nothing about who is good.

We combine both: every instructor listed here is an RSA-approved driving instructor (ADI), and where an instructor has a Google Business Profile we show their real Google rating, reviews and photos alongside. Pick your driving test centre, compare instructors nearby, and contact them directly by phone or email. We take no commission and instructors don't pay to be listed.
