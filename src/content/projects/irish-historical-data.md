Irish Historical Data is a collection of long-run datasets, each published with a chart and free to download as a CSV or Excel file.

The series covers schools, pupils and teachers from 1824 to 2026, railway route miles from 1834 to 2024, sheep and cattle at the Great October Fair of Ballinasloe from 1771 to 1952, and indictable crimes by county from 1871 to 1913. It also includes average sale prices, rents and gross yields for Dublin every year since 1945 and for Ireland since the 2000s.
