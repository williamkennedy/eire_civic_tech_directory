We built HomeTrue for people making real decisions about where they live.

Like a lot of people navigating the Irish housing market, we went through it the hard way. Viewings on a Saturday, decisions made under pressure — and the realisation too late that the commute was punishing, the broadband was unusable, and the nearest GP had a two-week wait. Whether you're buying or renting, the platforms you use are built for whoever is selling, not for you. HomeTrue exists to change that.
