MortgageLab.ie is a free mortgage toolkit that lets you check your borrowing power, compare rates from all 10 Irish lenders, and simulate your mortgage over the full term, including what happens when your fixed rate ends and how overpayments affect things. Rates are pulled daily, there's no ads or tracking, and everything runs locally in your browser.
