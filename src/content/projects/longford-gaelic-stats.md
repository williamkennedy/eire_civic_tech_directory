Longford Gaelic Stats is an Independent Digital Archive aimed at preserving over 135 years of Longford Gaelic Games statistics, records and insights for Club and County since 1890 across GAA Football & Hurling, Ladies Football, Camogie, Handball, Rounders & Masters Football. This archive is the result of 10 years of research using a variety of public domain sources. This digital archive was designed and developed by our volunteer research team, is wholly self-funded, fully independent and is not affiliated or associated with any GAA, LGFA or other organisation.
