It currently includes:
Theory:
- All driving theory questions (English + Arabic + more language support to come, I can add Irish too if wanted)
- mock tests + quick practice modes/games that are all tracked in the readiness score.
- all Irish road signs and hand signals
- interactive driving scenarios and animations to explain key topics where students fail.

Practical:
- “show me / tell me” and oral questions
- real test routes from all 55 RSA centres - Beta
- 2,500+ instructor search across Ireland
- a driving coach that uses phone sensors to detect harsh braking, speeding, sharp turns, etc. - Beta
- mirror-check detection using the front camera and AI. - Beta
- turn-by-turn guidance for test routes - Beta
- readiness tracking, EDT tracking, streaks, XP, and progress stats

It also works offline and installs like a normal app (PWA) and you can sign in with your Google account or email and enable Multi factor authentication and Passkey signing in.

I mainly built it because the whole learner-driver process in Ireland feels way more stressful and fragmented than it should be.
