MyIreland.ie is an interactive atlas of Ireland spanning 10,000 years of history. Visitors can drag a timeline from 8000 BC to the present day, exploring how kingdoms rose, Vikings landed, and republics were declared — county by county. The site also features dedicated sections on Irish mythology, saints and feast days, the Great Famine, hidden historical sites, and a surname map that lets users trace their clan's homeland across the island.
