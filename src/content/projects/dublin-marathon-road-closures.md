This site was created for the Irish Life Dublin Marathon on Sunday 25 October 2026. It shows what's closed right now and you can drag the slider to see any time on Sunday. You can also search your road or use the locate button to see what's near you. Tap a road and it tells you when it closes and reopens, with a link to Google Maps. If you want to send a specific road and time to someone there's a Copy link button that opens it exactly the way you're seeing it.
