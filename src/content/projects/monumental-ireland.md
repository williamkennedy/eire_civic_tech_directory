Monumental Ireland is a website and mobile app that maps Ireland's ancient monuments — court tombs, portal tombs, passage tombs, stone circles, ogham stones, hillforts, and more. Each entry includes a description, official National Monuments Service classification and SMR code, accessibility notes, images, and directions. Sites can be browsed by county or monument type.
