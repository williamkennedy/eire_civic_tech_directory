Dublin Crime Explorer maps official CSO recorded-crime data (CJA11 and CJQ06) across Dublin. It offers two geographies: 41 Garda station/sub-district areas with annual trends and 14 offence categories, and 6 Garda Divisions with real official boundary polygons, quarterly trends back to 2003, and the full 85-category official offence breakdown. The project is deliberately neutral — it shows counts, category mix and change over time without inventing a neighbourhood "safety score", and documents the limitations of the underlying data (station geography isn't suburb boundaries, recorded crime isn't total prevalence, fraud reporting gaps, etc).
