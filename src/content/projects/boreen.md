Boreen is a daily trivia game about Ireland. The name comes from the Irish *bóithrín*, a little country road.

Every day everyone gets the same seven questions across Irish life, geography, music & entertainment, history, sport, culture & language, and general knowledge. Each question has many right answers; you have 25 seconds per question, and rarer answers travel further (up to 700 km across seven questions). Journeys follow routes past Irish landmarks, with past days available in the archive. Free to play, with optional sign-in.
