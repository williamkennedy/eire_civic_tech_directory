BaaBaa.ie lists every childcare service on Tusla's register on a map, searchable by eircode or address, with each one's ages, service types and phone number.

Parents can apply online where the childcare accepts it and keep track of their applications. Childcares keep their waitlist online, share it with their team, print a poster and form with their QR code, and take applications online.

It is free for parents and childcares, paid for by contributions from people who use it.
