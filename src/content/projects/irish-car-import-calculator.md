What does it actually cost to import a car from the UK to Ireland?

The sticker price is rarely the price. Between VRT, VAT, customs duty and the NOx levy, a UK car can land a long way from what the ad says. This page explains what you pay — and the extension below puts the real figure on the listing while you browse.
