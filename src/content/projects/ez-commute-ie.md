EZ Commute IE brings live bus, Luas, and rail departures for all of Ireland into one simple app, not just Dublin.

FEATURES
• Live countdowns for Dublin Bus, Bus Éireann, DART, Irish Rail, and Luas
• Save your regular stops as favourites for one-tap access
• Search any stop or route across the country
• Tap the map to see live vehicle positions and full route paths
• Set a heads-up alarm before your bus or train departs, so you never run for it again
• Ghost bus reporting: community-powered warnings when a scheduled service never shows
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets to check departures without opening the app
• Full dark mode
• Gaeilge support, including Irish-language stop names
