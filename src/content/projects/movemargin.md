MoveMargin is a budgeting tool for people actively bidding on a home in Ireland. It answers one question: if I bid this much, what does it actually cost me — up front and every month?

It is not financial advice. MoveMargin does not assess your eligibility for a mortgage, does not apply any lender's criteria, and is not a substitute for talking to a broker, lender or solicitor.
