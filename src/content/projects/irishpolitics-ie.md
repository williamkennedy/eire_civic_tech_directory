irishpolitics.ie is an independent, non-partisan political data platform tracking opinion polls, seat projections, and key political developments in Ireland. We aggregate polling data from all three major Irish pollsters — Red C, Ireland Thinks, and Ipsos B&A — and present it in an accessible, visual format.

We are not affiliated with any political party, polling company, government body, or media organisation. Our goal is to make Irish political data more transparent and understandable for voters, journalists, researchers, and political enthusiasts.
