Rainy.ie is a simple website that fills the gap of easily understandable rain data in the Met Éireann app. It includes:

- A radar of all the rain in the past hour, without the flickering in the Met Eireann app that makes it difficult to tell the direction the rain is moving in
- A single slider that can take you from the past all the way into the future with Met Éireann's forecasts for the coming days

It's built using the great open data sets that Met Eireann publish online, it's completely free and you can easily install it on any iPhone or Android.
