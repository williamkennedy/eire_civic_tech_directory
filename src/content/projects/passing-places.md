Passing Places tells you the story of the place you are passing, as you pass it. It keeps a corpus of place-linked context for Ireland, watches what a moving person is approaching, chooses the one thing worth telling, and says it in a few grounded sentences. Then it goes quiet until the next place earns a word. It needs no destination, and it never tells the same story twice in one journey.
