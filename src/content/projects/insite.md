InSite pulls together the site-scouting categories a planner, PM, or architect would otherwise gather by hand — geology, water, heritage, environmental hazards, and planning context — from Ireland's public data sources, into one report.

It's free to use. Every data point is fetched live from its original public source (GSI, EPA, Tailte Éireann, the National Monuments Service, and others) — nothing is guessed or modelled beyond what's explicitly disclosed alongside each result.

InSite gives a fast first look at a site, not professional advice — see our terms of use.
