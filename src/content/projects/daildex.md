DáilDex is an independent tracker of TDs built from the official Oireachtas record. Follow your TDs with free, source-linked email alerts for Dáil votes, debates and parliamentary questions. Includes plain-English Irish politics guides and Ask Dex, a free civic AI assistant for questions about Irish government and the Oireachtas.
