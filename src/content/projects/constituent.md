Constituent is a pioneering platform dedicated to strengthening democratic engagement in Ireland. We believe that effective representation starts with accessible communication between citizens and their elected officials.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between citizens and their representatives by providing a user-friendly platform that makes it easy to find, connect with, and communicate with elected officials at all levels of government.

Whether you're looking to voice your concerns about local issues, engage with national policy discussions, or simply learn more about who represents you, our platform provides the tools and information you need to participate actively in democracy.
