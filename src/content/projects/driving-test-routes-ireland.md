It’s and android app called "Driving Test Routes Ireland" on play store, and I’ve basically done the manual work for you. I digitized the most common test routes for centres like Tallaght, Finglas, Navan, Raheny, and Mulhuddart (and more) so you don’t have to.

How it helps:
- The app locks you onto the specific test route, even if it’s not the most efficient way to go.
- You get actual voice instructions ("Turn left at the end of the road"), so you don't need a passenger screaming at you or to look at your phone.
- You can study the map before you drive.
- I threw in the "Show Me / Tell Me" checks and theory questions too, just to have everything in one place.
